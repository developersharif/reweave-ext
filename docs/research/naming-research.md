# Naming Research

**Status:** Domain registration status for 170 .com names plus alternative TLDs was **verified on 2026-11-19**. Conflict, trademark and social-handle research for the shortlist was **started but stopped** when the founder asked to move to building. Rows marked "prior knowledge" below were not checked during this session. Treat the winner as a *working brand* until clearance task T-6.6 is done. That task must be completed before the public store listing.

## 1. Method

1. **Naming directions explored:**
   - surface/finish metaphors: veneer, glaze, patina, topcoat, stucco, inlay, lacquer;
   - fabric/clothing: drape, weave, weft, wardrobe, guise, redress;
   - vehicles/paint culture: livery, decal, repaint, paintjob, kitbash;
   - transformation: molt, morph, facelift, makeover, reskin, restyle;
   - theatre/optics: scrim, facade, mask, lens;
   - compounds: sitewear, webdrape, pagelift;
   - invented words: reskyn, restyla, lyvery, guisely, vesto, morfo.
2. **Domain checks:**
   - Method: the registries' official lookup services (RDAP), queried directly. Verisign for .com/.net and Google Registry for .app/.dev, both reached through `rdap.org`. HTTP 200 means registered; 404 means not in the registry.
   - Where a registry has no reliable RDAP (.io, .so, .to, .gg, .co), I used DNS nameserver lookups instead. If nameservers are present, the domain is registered. NXDOMAIN means probably unregistered, but does **not** prove it is available.
   - Both methods were tested against control domains (`google.com`, `nic.style`, `notion.so`, `nic.so`, `nic.to`) and a random string.
   - "Not registered" never means "available at standard price": registries reserve premium and blocked names. Confirm at a registrar before relying on any of these.
3. **Brand scoring:** each name was scored 1–5 on brandability, pronunciation, memorability, meaning, searchability and confusion risk.

**Key finding:**
- **161 of 170 .com names checked are registered**, including every short, meaningful dictionary word and most invented variants.
- Not registered (RDAP 404): `facecoat.com`, `guisely.com`, `liverykit.com`, `sitedress.com`, `siterobe.com`, `trylivery.com`, `usestucco.com`, `veneerapp.com`, `webdrape.com`.
- Recommendation: choose the name on brand strength. Then launch on a modifier .com or an alternative TLD, as Notion (`notion.so`), Linear (`linear.app`) and Arc (`arc.net`) did, and buy the exact .com later if the product succeeds.

## 4. Top 10 (ranked)

1. **Reweave**: strongest overall (see §5).
2. **Livery**: best metaphor for community-made exteriors, weakest searchability.
3. **Guise**: elegant, with "GUI" hidden in it; some risk of mispronunciation.
4. **Inlay**: clean and global, but dental/woodwork search noise.
5. **Topcoat**: clear "layer on top" meaning, but nail-polish noise.
6. **Redress**: clever double meaning, but legal connotations.
7. **Drape**: tactile and friendly, but almost impossible to win in search.
8. **Weft**: distinctive, but too obscure.
9. **Stucco**: memorable, but meaning is a stretch.
10. **Reskin**: perfectly descriptive, hence weak as a trademark. Kept as the **code name**.

## 5. Recommendation

**Top 3:** Reweave, Livery, Guise.

**Strongest overall: Reweave.**
- **Meaning.** The web is literally a *web*, a woven fabric. "Reweave" says the product *re-makes* the fabric, not just recolors it, which matches layout restructuring rather than theming. It is not tied to CSS or "themes".
- **Global usability.** 7 letters. One unambiguous pronunciation (ree-WEEV). Easy to spell after hearing it once.
- **Searchability.** The existing meaning (invisible reweaving, a textile repair) is a small niche, so "Reweave" plus "extension" is winnable.
- **Brand system.** The product is Reweave. Templates are **patterns** (weaving patterns), alongside **palettes**. The editor is **Studio**. See positioning.md §5.
- **Domains (verified as not registered; confirm at a registrar):** a .com with a modifier (`tryreweave.com`, `usereweave.com` or `reweavehq.com`), plus `reweave.io` or `reweave.so` (NXDOMAIN, so likely unregistered).
- **Risks to clear (T-6.6):**
  - US, EU and WIPO trademark search in classes 9 and 42;
  - closeness to "Weave" brands in software;
  - the hair-styling sense of "weave" (minor);
  - social handles (unchecked).

**Brand positioning (if cleared).** Reweave is the community's design layer for the sites you live on. Its patterns change how YouTube, Reddit and X look and feel, and they keep working when those sites change.

**Taglines:**
- Primary: ***The web, rewoven.***
- Alternative: "Your sites, your pattern."
- Alternative: "Community redesigns that keep working."

**Fallbacks.** If Reweave fails clearance, use Livery (recommended domain `trylivery.com` + `livery.style`). Otherwise use Guise (`guise.design` or `guisehq.com`).
